Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Digital Solutions Team ("we", "us") collects through this website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We keep it as short and plain as we can.

1. Who we are

Digital Solutions Team is a web, mobile, AI and integration services team based at Door No. 70-1-4/4, 2nd floor, Polisetty Enclave, Sarpavaram, Andhra Pradesh 533005, India. For anything in this policy, write to info@digitalsolutions.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, email address and the project details you type into the message box.
  • Newsletter: your email address, when you subscribe.
  • Direct contact: details you share by email, phone or WhatsApp when you message us.
  • Chat assistant: the questions you type into the chat widget are answered automatically in your browser. The conversation is kept in your browser's session storage until you close the tab or start a new chat, and it is not sent to us. If you choose to leave your details in the chat (name, email or phone, and project description), they are sent to us through the same form service as the enquiry form. If you tap a WhatsApp button, WhatsApp opens with a short pre-filled message and WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply from there. Please do not enter passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.
  • Project estimator: the estimator runs in your browser. Nothing from it is saved or sent to us unless you choose to send it with an enquiry.
  • Technical data: basic information your browser sends when you visit, such as IP address, browser type, device type and pages viewed. This is usually recorded by our hosting provider in standard server logs. Your browser may also store small settings such as the light or dark theme you picked.

We do not ask for, and you should not send us, government ID numbers, bank or card details through the website forms.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote or proposal.
  • To send the newsletter, if you subscribed.
  • To run, secure and improve the website and the chat assistant.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We rely on your consent (for the newsletter and for information you choose to send us), on steps taken at your request before a contract (quotes and proposals), and on our legitimate interest in keeping the website working and secure. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the website, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Formspree (formspree.io) receives and forwards the contact and newsletter form submissions to us.
  • Google provides the embedded map and web fonts. Loading them lets Google see your IP address and browser details.
  • Content delivery networks (jsDelivr, cdnjs) deliver Bootstrap, Font Awesome and similar files, and see your IP address when your browser requests them.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) handles messages if you choose to contact us through the WhatsApp link, under its own privacy policy.
  • AI service providers. If the chat assistant uses an external AI service to generate replies, the text you type may be sent to that provider for that purpose.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the public from fraud or abuse.

5. Where your information is stored

Some of these providers are located outside India, so your information may be processed in other countries. We choose providers that apply reasonable security safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects are kept for up to 24 months so we can follow up. Information about clients we work with is kept for as long as the project runs and for the period tax and accounting law requires afterwards. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

7. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and, where it applies to you, laws such as the GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you,
  • correct or update it,
  • delete it, and
  • withdraw consent you gave us earlier.

To use any of these rights, or to unsubscribe from the newsletter, email info@digitalsolutions.com. We will reply within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our response, you may also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not use advertising cookies. It may store small preference settings in your browser, and the third-party services listed above may set their own technical cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may then work less well. If we add analytics tools in future, we will update this policy.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited access to submitted data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

This website is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Links to other sites

The website links to client projects, social media and other third-party sites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed, and significant changes will be flagged on the website.
